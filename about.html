About Erica

My Story

Hi, I'm Erica Bowling. I am a student at Aveda Institute in Indianapolis. I enjoy all aspects of hair design with a special passion for cutting and coloring. Beauty is a passion of mine because there is beauty in all things and I love having the opportunity to bring out the beauty in others. I am looking for clients for makeup, hair styling, coloring, perming, and relaxing. I would love the opportunity to work on you to highlight your beauty! As I grow I am excited to offer more and more services in the cosmetology industry.
